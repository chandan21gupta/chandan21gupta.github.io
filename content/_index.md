I’m **Chandan Gupta**, a researcher at **IIT Delhi** working with **Dr. N. M. Anoop Krishnan**. My current work is on self-improving agents for scientific problems where success is difficult to verify, including material synthesis.

My research has taken me across scientific domains: from learning physical dynamics during an internship at **Czech Technical University in Prague**, to inferring human aging trajectories with the **University of Cambridge**, and now to evaluating and developing scientific agents at IIT Delhi. Across these projects, I’m interested in how machine learning can help investigate complex scientific systems.

### Selected research

- **Scientific agents:** Co-developed Corral, an evaluation of scientific agents across eight domains and 25,000+ runs. Presented orally at the **NeurIPS 2025 AI for Science Workshop**. [Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=OLJp4ndI7i).
- **Computational biology:** Inferred human aging trajectories from cross-sectional DNA methylation data. Accepted at **MLCB 2026**. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06583).
- **Scientific machine learning:** Developed symplectic neural methods for learning physical dynamics and generalized Hamiltonians. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27029).

### Applied machine learning

In an applied machine learning role, I was an **Associate Machine Learning Scientist at Wadhwani AI** (Dec 2022–Jun 2024). I worked on systems for agriculture, including crop disease identification, yield estimation, and advisory tools, as well as model monitoring and deployment.

My work on the **Counter Turing Test (CT2)** received the Outstanding Paper Award in the Resources and Evaluation track at **EMNLP 2023**. [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.136/).
