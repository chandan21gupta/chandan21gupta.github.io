I’m **Chandan Gupta**, a researcher at **IIT Delhi** working with **Dr. N. M. Anoop Krishnan**. My current work is on self-improving agents for non-verifiable domains, such as materials science.

I’m interested in **AI for Science**: how AI can enable autonomous scientific discovery. My research has taken me across physics, biology, and materials science, with research experience at the **University of Cambridge** and **Czech Technical University in Prague**.

See my [selected publications](/publications/) or visit [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=GVQrnLsAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate) for the full list.

Earlier, I worked in industry as an **Associate Machine Learning Scientist I at Wadhwani AI** (Dec 2022–Jun 2024), engineering AI for social good in agriculture. I built uncertainty-aware crop disease and pest models, crop-yield predictions, and a causal advisory system for farmers. I also developed data-drift monitoring and deployed research models through NVIDIA Triton for cloud and Android field applications.

For the broader questions that connect these projects, see [Research](/research/).
