I’m **Chandan Gupta**, a researcher at **IIT Delhi** working with **Dr. N. M. Anoop Krishnan**. I work on AI and machine learning, with a current focus on self-improving agents for scientific discovery and material synthesis.

### Current research

At IIT Delhi, I co-developed **Corral**, an open-source framework for evaluating scientific agents across eight domains and more than 25,000 agent runs. This work found that agents often ignore contradictory evidence, highlighting why success metrics alone can miss important reasoning failures. The work was presented orally at the **NeurIPS 2025 AI for Science Workshop**; the full paper is under review. [Read the paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=OLJp4ndI7i).

I’m also working on self-improving agents that evolve their own harnesses to propose experimental protocols for synthesizing target materials.
