*Machine learning engineer · AI researcher · AI for Science*

I’m **Chandan Gupta**, a researcher at **IIT Delhi** working with **Dr. N. M. Anoop Krishnan**. My current work is on self-improving agents for non-verifiable domains, such as materials science.

I’m interested in **AI for Science**: how AI can enable autonomous scientific discovery. My research has taken me across physics, biology, and materials science, with research experience at the **University of Cambridge** and **Czech Technical University in Prague**. Earlier, at **Wadhwani AI**, I worked on engineering machine learning systems for agriculture and public benefit—from crop surveillance and yield estimation to field deployment—as part of AI for social good.

See below for my **selected publications**; for my full publication list, visit [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=GVQrnLsAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate).

### Applied machine learning

At **Wadhwani AI**, where I was an **Associate Machine Learning Scientist I** (Dec 2022–Jun 2024), I engineered machine learning systems for agricultural use and social impact. I built uncertainty-aware crop pest and disease identification models for the National Pest Surveillance Service, using Regularized Adaptive Prediction Sets to make predictions more reliable in real-world conditions. I also developed crop-yield models from soil, weather, and management data, alongside a causal advisory system that offered farmers tailored recommendations.

Beyond the models themselves, I built an automated data-drift monitoring pipeline to flag performance changes and support timely retraining. I also productionized research models with NVIDIA Triton Inference Server, making them available to cloud and Android applications used in the field.
