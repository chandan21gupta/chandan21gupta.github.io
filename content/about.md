I see this website in two ways:

1. A collection of my ideas, questions, writing, reading, reflections, and curious experiments.

2. A way to share them. If something interests you—a question, an experiment, or an idea you agree or disagree with—feel free to get in touch.

### Background

I studied Computer Science and Biosciences at the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi, graduating in 2022. During my undergraduate years, I became interested in using machine learning to understand scientific and real-world problems.

In 2022, I interned at Czech Technical University in Prague, where I worked on learning physical dynamics from data. After graduating, I joined Wadhwani AI as an Associate Machine Learning Scientist. From 2022 to 2024, I helped build and deploy machine learning systems for agriculture, including crop disease identification, yield estimation, and farmer advisory tools.

In 2024, I began research collaborations in computational biology with the University of Cambridge, working on human aging trajectories from DNA methylation data. I also joined IIT Delhi as a Research Assistant, where I now work with Dr. N. M. Anoop Krishnan on AI for Science, including scientific-agent evaluation and self-improving agents for material synthesis.
