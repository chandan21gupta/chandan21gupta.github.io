I see this website in two ways:

1. A collection of my ideas, questions, writing, reading, reflections, and curious experiments.

2. A way to share them. If something interests you—a question, an experiment, or an idea you agree or disagree with—feel free to get in touch.

### Background

I studied Computer Science and Biosciences at the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi, graduating in 2022. During my undergraduate years, I became interested in using machine learning to understand scientific and real-world problems.

I joined Wadhwani AI as an Associate Machine Learning Scientist, working on the engineering needed to take machine-learning models into production. I built an automated data-drift monitoring pipeline to help detect model degradation and trigger timely retraining, and deployed research models with NVIDIA Triton so they could be used by cloud and Android applications in the field. These systems supported agricultural applications, including crop pest and disease identification, yield estimation, and farmer advisory tools.

In 2024, I joined IIT Delhi as a Research Assistant, where I now work with Dr. N. M. Anoop Krishnan on scientific-agent evaluation and self-improving agents in AI for Science.
