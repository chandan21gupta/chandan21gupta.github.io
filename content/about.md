I see this website in two ways:

1. A collection of my ideas, questions, writing, reading, reflections, and curious experiments.

2. A way to share them. If something interests you—a question, an experiment, or an idea you agree or disagree with—feel free to get in touch.

### Background

I studied Computer Science and Biosciences at the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi, graduating in 2022.

I joined Wadhwani AI as an Associate Machine Learning Scientist, working on **AI for social good** by helping farmers detect crop pests early and access AI-based yield advice. I also worked on the systems behind these tools, including model-drift monitoring and improving prediction reliability by incorporating conformal predictive sets.

In 2024, I joined IIT Delhi as a Research Assistant, where I now work with Dr. N. M. Anoop Krishnan on scientific-agent evaluation and self-improving agents in AI for Science.
